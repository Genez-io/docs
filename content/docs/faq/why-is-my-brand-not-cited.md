# Why is my brand not cited?

Almost always one of four reasons, in this order of likelihood.

---

## 1. There is nothing to cite

Engines cite sources that answer the question. If your site positions and
persuades but never answers the specific question being asked, there is nothing
to lift.

**Check:** read the conversations for a topic where you are absent and look at
what *was* cited. Compare it with your equivalent page. The difference is
usually that theirs answers and yours sells.

## 2. Everything about you is written by you

Engines weight independent sources more heavily, because a brand describing
itself is weak evidence. A citation profile made only of your own pages is
fragile and easy to displace.

**Check:** look at [Most Cited Sources](../insights/most-cited-sources.html).
If your own domain is the only place you appear, that is the finding.

## 3. Your page cannot be lifted from

Answer engines quote passages. A page where the substance is spread across
marketing copy, or locked in an image, a PDF or a video with no transcript,
gives them nothing quotable even when the information is there.

**Check:** could you copy one self-contained paragraph that answers the
question? If not, neither can an engine.

## 4. You are answering a different question

The question you optimised for and the question buyers ask are often not the
same. [Query fanouts](../analysis/extracting-query-fanouts.html) show what
retrieval actually looks for.

---

## What not to conclude

Not being cited is not a penalty, and there is nothing to appeal. Engines are
not withholding you; they found better material for that question. The work is
to be the better material.
