# How accurate are AI visibility scores?

Accurate as a measurement of the questions you asked, on the engines you chose,
in the period you ran. Not accurate as a universal statement about your brand —
and no such number exists.

---

## What the score genuinely represents

Real answers, from real engines, to questions you defined. Nothing is modelled
or estimated: every point is built from conversations you can open and read.

That is its strength. When a figure surprises you, you can go and look at the
answers behind it.

---

## What it depends on

**Your question set.** Change the scenarios and the number changes. This is
correct behaviour — you are measuring a different thing — but it means the
score is not comparable across brands with different topics.

**Your engines.** Visibility varies a lot between engines. A blended number
across four is an average of four different positions.

**Sampling.** Answer engines vary run to run, so any single reading carries
noise. See [Why do LLM results change?](why-do-llm-results-change.html).

---

## How to use it so it stays honest

**Compare with yourself over time.** The most reliable use. Same questions,
same engines, different weeks.

**Compare with competitors on the same set.** Valid, because everyone is
measured on identical questions.

**Do not compare against an industry benchmark.** 40% in a crowded category
can be strong and 40% in a niche weak. A number from another brand's topic set
is not a benchmark for yours.

---

## The honest summary

Treat it as an instrument reading, not a fact: directionally reliable, precise
to a few points, and always explicable by opening the conversations underneath.
