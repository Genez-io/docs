# How often should I run conversations?

**Weekly is right for most brands.** Often enough to catch a real change
within days, infrequent enough that you are reading signal rather than noise.

---

## Why not daily

Answer engines vary between runs. Day-to-day movement is mostly the medium,
not your market, and watching it daily trains you to react to nothing.

Daily is worth it in two situations: the week after a significant launch, and
when you are actively testing whether a change worked.

---

## Why not monthly

A month is long enough for a competitor to publish something, get cited, and
take a position before you notice. By the time you see it, the explanation has
scrolled out of easy reach.

---

## The real constraint

Your plan sets how many conversations you can run. The question is usually not
"how often" but "how to spend the budget":

- **More topics, less often** gives breadth — better when you are mapping a
  category.
- **Fewer topics, more often** gives a reliable trend on the questions that
  matter — better when you are working on something specific.

Breadth first while you learn the shape of your category, then narrow.

---

## A practical pattern

Weekly across your tracked topics, plus an extra run after anything you expect
to move the numbers. Compare like with like: a run after a publication is only
meaningful against a run before it.
