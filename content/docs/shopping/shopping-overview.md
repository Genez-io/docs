# Shopping Overview

The **Shopping Overview** is the dashboard for product-level performance. It is the first page you open in the shopping section and the one you'll use for weekly checks and monthly reporting.

Where the brand dashboards answer *"how is my brand doing in AI answers?"*, Shopping Overview answers *"which of my products are doing well, and which are being left out?"* — see [Product Visibility](product-visibility.html) for what product-level tracking is and why it matters.

Shopping is an **enterprise feature**, enabled for a brand by the Genezio team.

---

## What the Dashboard Shows

### The Three Metrics

At the top of the page you'll find the headline numbers:

* **Product Visibility** — how often your products appear in eligible conversations.
* **Product Recommendation** — of the conversations where a product appears, how often it is actually recommended.
* **Share of Voice** — how much of the product conversation is yours versus the competing products around it.

These use the same formulas as the brand-level KPIs, scoped to products. See [Insights -> Share of Voice](../insights/share-of-voice.html) for how share is interpreted.

### Top 10 Leaderboards

For each of the three metrics, the dashboard shows a **Top 10 product leaderboard** — your ten strongest products by that metric.

### Trend Charts

Each metric has a **trend chart** showing how it has moved across the selected period, so you can see direction rather than a single snapshot.

### Period-over-Period Change

Every metric shows a **percentage change versus the previous period**, so you immediately see whether things are improving, flat, or slipping.

### Total Retailers Card

A **total-retailers card** shows how many retailers are associated with your products across the conversations Genezio analyzed. Drill into the detail on [Merchants and Retailers](merchants-and-retailers.html).

## How to Read It

**Start with the three metrics, in order.** Visibility tells you reach. Recommendation tells you whether that reach converts into advocacy. Share of Voice tells you how you sit against the competing products. A drop in Visibility and a drop in Recommendation are different problems and need different fixes.

**Read the leaderboards as three different questions.** The same product rarely tops all three lists, and the differences are the insight:

* Top by **Visibility** — the products AI mentions most. These carry your presence.
* Top by **Recommendation** — the products AI actively advocates for. These are your strongest performers, even if they're mentioned less often.
* Top by **Share of Voice** — the products that own their competitive space.

The most useful comparison is what's *missing*. If the product you're currently promoting is not on any leaderboard, AI isn't carrying your campaign — that's a content and source problem, not a media problem.

**Treat a period-over-period swing as a prompt to investigate, not a conclusion.** A large PoP move usually means one of three things: something changed in your content or coverage, a competitor gained or lost ground, or the set of detected products shifted because answer engines started naming something new. Check the trend chart to see whether the move is a spike or a sustained direction, then open [Products](products.html) to see which specific products drove it.

**Remember where the products come from.** Products are auto-detected from AI conversations only — there is no catalog upload or feed. So the dashboard reflects the products answer engines actually talk about, not your full catalog.

## Ask Geo from the Cards

The shopping cards include **one-click contextual questions** wired to your actual numbers. Rather than reading a metric and wondering what to do about it, you click a question and get an answer grounded in your data — for example:

> **We have 0% visibility — where do we start?**

The question carries the context of the card it came from, which turns a number on a dashboard into a next action. Full detail on what Geo can do is in [GEO Assistant](../geo-assistant/geo-assistant.html).
