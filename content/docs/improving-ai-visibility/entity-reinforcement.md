# Entity Reinforcement

An answer engine has to know *what you are* before it can decide whether you
answer a question. Entity reinforcement is making that unambiguous.

---

## The problem it solves

Brands are often ambiguous to a machine:

- The name is also a common word, or another company.
- The category is described differently in different places — "project
  management", "work OS", "collaboration platform".
- The product has changed and older sources describe the old one.

An engine resolving you inconsistently will sometimes answer about something
else, and sometimes leave you out of answers you belong in.

---

## What reinforcement looks like

**Say what you are, plainly, in the words buyers use.** Positioning language
that avoids the category name makes you harder to place. "The operating system
for modern teams" tells a machine nothing.

**Say it consistently everywhere.** Your site, your profiles, your
documentation. Contradiction makes engines hedge.

**Use structured markup.** Schema tells engines what a page is rather than
making them infer it. See [Schema Markup](../content-hub/schema-markup.html).

**Disambiguate deliberately.** If your name collides, pair it with the category
in prose that is likely to be retrieved.

---

## How to tell whether it is working

Read your conversations. If engines describe your category inconsistently, or
confuse you with another brand, that is an entity problem — and no amount of
content on specific questions fixes it.

This is foundational work: worth doing before chasing individual topics,
because everything else assumes the engine knows which company you are.
