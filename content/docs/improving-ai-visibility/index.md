# Improving AI Visibility

Strategy, in rough order of leverage.

[How LLMs Choose Sources](how-llms-choose-sources.html) explains the mechanism:
retrieval, then trust, then quoting. Each stage fails differently.

The cheapest work is usually
[structuring what you already have](structuring-content-for-llms.html) so a
passage can be lifted, and [updating pages](updating-content.html) that are
already retrieved.

The durable work is [building authority](building-authority.html) through
independent sources — and
[Backlinks and Citations](backlinks-and-citations.html) explains why that is not
the same as link building.

The foundations are [entity reinforcement](entity-reinforcement.html), so
engines know what you are, and
[semantic topic coverage](semantic-topic-coverage.html), so a topic is covered
in substance rather than in name.

Then [monitor it](monitoring-ai-visibility.html) without reading noise as a
result.
