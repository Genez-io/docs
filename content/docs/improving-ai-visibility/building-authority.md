# Building Authority

Engines prefer independent sources, because a brand describing itself is not
evidence. Authority here means being described by others, in places engines
retrieve.

---

## Why your own site has a ceiling

You can write a perfect page and still lose to a mediocre third-party
comparison, because the comparison is evidence and your page is a claim.

A citation profile made only of your own domain is also fragile: one competitor
earning independent coverage can displace it. See
[Most Cited Sources](../insights/most-cited-sources.html).

---

## Where authority comes from

**Review and comparison sites**, where buyers in your category already look.
Usually the highest-leverage place, because they are cited heavily and your
profile is partly in your control.

**Community discussion** — the subreddit or forum where your category is
argued about. Engines cite these, and they cannot be bought, only earned.

**Independent writers and creators** covering your space. Where Genezio can
identify them, it names the specific channel or author — see
[Channels and Authors](../insights/channels-and-authors.html). That turns
outreach from a guess into a list.

**Documentation and technical writing** that others reference. Slow, durable,
and the only route some categories have.

---

## How to choose where to spend

Start from what is already cited in your category rather than from a general
list of good publications. A site cited forty times for your questions is worth
more than a prestigious one cited never.

---

## What does not work

Paid placements that engines do not retrieve. Press releases nobody picks up.
Link building aimed at search rankings — answer engines are reading the text,
not counting the link.
