# Extracting Query Fanouts

When somebody asks an answer engine a question, the engine rarely searches for
that exact sentence. It expands it into several narrower queries, retrieves for
those, and composes an answer.

Those expansions are query fanouts, and Genezio captures them.

---

## Why they are worth having

A fanout is the question the engine actually went looking for — closer to a
retrieval query than to the human sentence that prompted it.

That makes them the most direct description available of what a page has to
answer in order to be found. A page written against the human question is
aiming at the wrong target; a page written against the fanouts is aiming at
what retrieval will match.

---

## What they tell you

**The vocabulary of the demand.** Fanouts use the words the engine expects to
find in a good source. If your page uses different language for the same idea,
it is harder to retrieve.

**The sub-questions you have to cover.** One buyer question frequently expands
into five. A page answering one of the five is incomplete in the engine's view,
however well it answers that one.

**Where you are close but missing.** Some fanouts you nearly match. Those are
usually cheaper to win than the ones you miss entirely.
