# Analysis

How Genezio produces the data: what it asks, where it asks, and what it reads
out of the answers.

Set up [scenarios](creating-scenarios.html) and
[answer engines](selecting-answer-engines.html), then
[run conversations](running-conversations.html).

From each answer Genezio extracts [citations](detecting-citations.html),
[competitors](extracting-competitors.html),
[query fanouts](extracting-query-fanouts.html) and
[sentiment](sentiment-analysis.html).

The page most people should read first is
[Understanding Conversation Results](understanding-conversation-results.html).
Scores tell you where to look; answers tell you why, and the why is where the
work is.
