# Extracting Competitors

Genezio finds your competitors in the answers themselves — the brands engines
actually name beside you — rather than relying only on a list you supply.

---

## Why discovery matters

The brands an answer engine puts beside you are not always the ones your sales
team names. Engines surface brands according to how well sources support them,
not by market share or by who you lose deals to.

So the list frequently contains a surprise: a brand you had not considered that
an engine treats as a direct alternative. That is worth knowing early, because
a competitor that exists in AI answers today tends to arrive in your pipeline
later.

---

## How it works

Every conversation is read for brand names appearing as alternatives, and those
are collected across runs. A brand named once is noise; one named consistently
across topics is a real competitor in the eyes of the engines.

You can also add competitors you want tracked regardless of whether engines
surface them, which is the right move for a rival you know matters even if they
are currently invisible.

---

## Keeping the list honest

**Remove the ones that are not competitors.** Engines sometimes name a adjacent
category or a parent company. Leaving those in distorts
[Share of Voice](../dashboards/share-of-voice.html), because share is divided
among whoever is on the list.

**Watch for name collisions.** A brand whose name is also a common word can
be over-counted. If a competitor's figures look implausible, this is usually
why.

**Revisit it.** The set of brands an engine considers alternatives changes.
A list fixed at setup slowly stops describing the category.
