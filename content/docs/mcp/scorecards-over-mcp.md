# Scorecards over MCP

You can create and change Business Scorecard goals from an MCP client, without
opening the board.

---

## Why do it there

The board is the right place to *read* goals. But goals are usually decided in
the middle of another conversation — a planning session, a quarterly review, a
competitor analysis you are already running in your AI client.

Creating the goal where the decision is made means it exists before anybody
forgets. You say:

> Add a goal: be recommended for "project management for agencies" on ChatGPT
> and Perplexity, UK only, measured against Asana and Monday.

and the goal is on the board, tracking from that day.

---

## What happens

The same agent that writes goals in the dashboard writes them here. It reads
your brand, works out what to measure, and builds the goal. You get back what
it is going to track, in words, and you accept it or keep refining — exactly as
you would in the board's own conversation.

A goal created from an MCP client is a normal goal. It appears on the board, it
is measured every day, you can open its card and follow the number back to the
conversations behind it, and you can tag, reorder or archive it like any other.

---

## What you can do

- **Create a goal** by describing it.
- **Read the board** — what each goal is and where it stands.
- **Change a goal** — widen the market, swap a competitor, change the engines.
