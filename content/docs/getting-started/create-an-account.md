# Create an Account

An account holds your brands, your team and your plan. Most companies need
exactly one.

---

## Signing up

Create an account from the Genezio sign-up page. The person who creates it
becomes its **owner**, which matters later: some actions — API keys, plan
changes — are reserved for owners.

If your company already has an account, do not create a second one. Ask an
owner to invite you instead. Two accounts means two sets of brands, two sets of
numbers, and no way to compare them.

---

## Signing in

Email and password by default. Larger organisations can sign in through their
own identity provider; see
[Enterprise SSO and SCIM](../security/enterprise-sso-and-scim.html).

---

## Inviting your team

Invite colleagues from **Settings → Users**. A member can be given the whole
account or **limited to specific brands**, which is the right setting for:

- an agency giving a client access to that client's brand only
- a regional team that should see its own market and not the others
- an external contractor working on one product line

A member limited to some brands sees only those brands everywhere in the
product.
