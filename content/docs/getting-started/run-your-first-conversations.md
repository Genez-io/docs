# Run Your First Conversations

A conversation is one scenario put to one answer engine. Running a set of them
is how every number in Genezio gets made.

---

## Before you run

Check three things, because they determine whether the results mean anything:

- **Your topics are ones you would act on.** See
  [Define Topics](define-topics.html).
- **Each topic has scenarios that sound like real questions.** See
  [Creating Scenarios](../analysis/creating-scenarios.html).
- **Your competitors list is roughly right.** It can be corrected later, but
  wildly wrong entries distort share figures from the start.

---

## Choosing answer engines

Pick the engines your buyers actually use. Genezio supports ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok and DeepSeek, as well as Google AI Overview.

More engines means more conversations against your budget, so start with two or
three that matter for your market rather than all of them. See
[Selecting Answer Engines](../analysis/selecting-answer-engines.html).

---

## What a run costs

Conversations multiply: topics × scenarios × engines. Ten topics with ten
scenarios each on four engines is four hundred conversations per run. Check the
arithmetic before starting, because the number grows faster than people expect.

---

## While it runs

Answers arrive as they complete; you do not have to wait for the whole run.
Reading the first few is worthwhile — a badly phrased scenario is obvious
immediately and is much cheaper to fix before the full set completes.

---

## When it finishes

Go to [Understanding Your First Results](understanding-your-first-results.html).
Resist drawing conclusions from the headline score until you have read some
actual answers.
