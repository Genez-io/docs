# Create Your First Brand

A brand is the unit everything else attaches to: topics, competitors,
conversations, content and scores all belong to one.

---

## What counts as a brand

Usually your company. But the right unit is **the thing buyers ask about**, and
that is not always the company:

- A company with one product: one brand.
- A company with distinct products buyers evaluate separately: one brand each.
- A company operating in several markets or languages: one brand per market.

The last is the one teams get wrong most often. A single brand spanning two
languages produces one blurred average instead of two usable pictures, because
the questions, the competitors and the sources are all different.

---

## What you need

**The name.** As buyers write it. If people shorten it, note that — it affects
what counts as a mention.

**The website.** The primary domain. Genezio uses it to understand what you do
and to recognise citations of your own pages.

**The language and market.** These shape the questions asked and the
competitors found.

---

## Describing the brand well

The description is used to generate topics and to judge relevance, so it is
worth writing properly.

Describe **what you do and who for**, in the words a customer would use. Avoid
internal positioning language. "Project management for creative agencies" works
far better than "the operating system for modern teams", because the first
matches how somebody would ask.
