# Define Topics

A topic is a subject buyers ask answer engines about. Topics decide everything
Genezio measures, so a weak topic set produces numbers that look fine and mean
nothing.

---

## What makes a good topic

**It is a question somebody would actually ask.** Not a product feature, not an
internal category name.

**It is narrow enough to win or lose.** "Software" is unmeasurable. "CRM for
small sales teams" has an answer, and you are either in it or not.

**It matters commercially.** Visibility on a topic nobody buys from is a vanity
number.

---

## Where to start

Genezio proposes topics from your brand description and website. Treat those as
a draft. The ones worth keeping are the ones you recognise from sales calls.

Three sources of good topics:

- **The questions prospects actually ask you.** Your sales team has these.
- **The comparisons you lose.** If buyers weigh you against a competitor, that
  comparison is a topic.
- **Your Search Console queries**, if connected — real demand you already
  receive. See
  [Google Search Console](../integrations/google-search-console.html).

---

## How many

Start with **five to ten**. Enough to see the shape of your category, few
enough that each gets a reliable number within your conversation budget.

Spreading a small budget across forty topics gives forty unreliable readings.
Breadth is a later problem; start with the topics you would act on.

---

## What topics are not

Topics are not keywords. A keyword is a string somebody types into a search
box; a topic is a subject somebody raises with an assistant, and the assistant
expands it into several queries of its own. See
[Query Fanouts](../core-concepts/query-fanouts.html).
