# Topic Performance

One visibility number for a brand hides the only thing you can act on: you are
strong on some topics and absent on others.

Topic performance is that breakdown.

---

## What to look for

**Topics where you score zero.** The most actionable rows. A zero means engines
answer that question without you at all, and the conversations will show who
they answer it with instead.

**Topics where you are visible but not recommended.** You come up and are passed
over. That is a positioning problem, and the answers usually say why.

**Topics that are moving.** A topic sliding over several runs is worth more
attention than a topic that has always been weak — something changed recently,
and recent changes are the easiest to trace.

---

## Sorting by what matters

A topic with high demand and low visibility is worth more than a topic with
low demand you happen to lead. Sort by the gap, not by the score.

Your worst topic is not automatically your best opportunity: some questions
are ones you should not win, because the honest answer is a competitor.

---

## Going deeper

Open a topic to see the scenarios under it and the conversations each one
produced. The conversation is where the explanation lives — the score tells you
where to look, the answer tells you why.
