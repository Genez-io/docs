# Citation Frequency

Which sources answer engines lean on in your category, and how often.

---

## What the number means

A citation count is how many times a source was used to support an answer in
your conversations. A source cited forty times is shaping your category; one
cited twice is noise.

Rank matters more than the absolute figure. The absolute number depends on how
many conversations you ran; the order does not.

---

## Reading the list

**The top of the list is your real competition for attention.** Not the brands
— the sources. If a comparison site sits at the top, your position in the
category is substantially decided by what that site says.

**Your own domain's position is diagnostic.** High is good but fragile: engines
weight independent sources more heavily, because a brand describing itself is
weak evidence. A profile made only of your own pages can be displaced by one
competitor earning third-party coverage.

**Absences are findings.** A publication you expected and do not see is one an
engine does not trust or cannot parse for this category.

---

## From a domain to something you can do

A domain is often too coarse. Where Genezio can tell who published the cited
material, it resolves to the channel or author — the specific YouTube channel,
the subreddit, the named writer. See
[Channels and Authors](../insights/channels-and-authors.html).

That is the difference between "do something about YouTube" and "this channel,
eleven times, is worth an email".
