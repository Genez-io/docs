# Share of Voice

Share of Voice is how much of the conversation in your category is about you
rather than anyone else.

> mentions of your brand ÷ mentions of all brands, yours and competitors × 100

---

## What makes it different from visibility

[Visibility](visibility-score-explained.html) asks whether you appear.
Share of Voice asks how much of the space you take when you do.

The two move independently, and the gap is informative. Appearing in most
answers while holding a small share means you are mentioned in passing while
somebody else is discussed properly.

---

## Reading it

**It is zero-sum.** Your share can fall while your visibility rises, if a
competitor grew faster. That is not a failure of your work; it is the category
getting busier, and it is worth saying so when you report it.

**Watch the leader's share, not just yours.** A category where the leader holds
60% behaves differently from one where the top three hold 20% each. The first
is a position to attack; the second is a field still open.

**A new entrant takes share from somebody.** When your share drops, check
whether a brand appeared that was not there before.

---

## Where it comes from

Mentions are counted from the same conversations behind every other number, so
Share of Voice is always consistent with what you can read in
[Conversations](../core-concepts/conversations.html). When a figure surprises
you, open the answers underneath it.
