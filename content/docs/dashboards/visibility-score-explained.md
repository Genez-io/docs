# Visibility Score Explained

The visibility figure on your dashboard is the share of relevant conversations
your brand appeared in at all.

> conversations where your brand appears ÷ total eligible conversations × 100

---

## Reading the card

The number is the current period. The movement beside it is against the
previous one — that is usually the more useful half, because a score only means
something in motion.

The card is an average across every topic and every answer engine you track. It
is the right number for a board slide and the wrong number for deciding what to
do on Monday, because it hides where the problem is.

---

## When it moves and nothing changed

Answer engines are not deterministic. Ask the same question twice and the
wording, and sometimes the brands, will differ. A few points of movement
between runs is the normal noise of the medium.

Treat a single reading as an estimate and a sustained direction over several
runs as a finding.

---

## Before you act on it

Break it down before you spend anything:

- **By topic** — you are rarely weak everywhere. See
  [Topic Performance](topic-performance.html).
- **By answer engine** — a brand strong on one engine and absent on another has
  a source problem specific to that engine.
- **Against competitors** — 40% means nothing until you know whether the leader
  is at 45% or 90%.
