# Improve AI Visibility for a SaaS Company

A worked sequence for software, where the buying question is usually a
comparison.

---

## 1. Topics are the comparisons you lose

Your sales team already knows them. "CRM for agencies", "alternative to
[competitor]", "tool for teams of five to fifty". Start with ten.

See [Define Topics](../getting-started/define-topics.html).

## 2. Run and read before scoring

Read a dozen answers. For SaaS you are looking for:

- Which competitors engines treat as your real alternatives — often not the
  ones you track commercially
- How you are characterised when named: expensive, complex, for enterprises
- Whether answers cite comparison sites, docs, or community threads

## 3. Deal with characterisation before coverage

A brand named in most answers and recommended in few has a positioning
problem, and more content will not fix it.
[Monitor Perceptions](../insights/monitor-perceptions.html) the specific claim
— "hard to set up", "expensive" — so you can tell whether work against it
lands.

## 4. Make your existing pages quotable

SaaS sites usually have the information and bury it in marketing copy. Apply
the test in
[Structuring Content for LLMs](../improving-ai-visibility/structuring-content-for-llms.html)
to pricing, integrations and comparison pages. This is the cheapest work
available.

## 5. Earn independent coverage

Engines weight third parties more heavily. For software that means review
platforms, comparison sites and the communities where your category is
discussed. See [Building Authority](../improving-ai-visibility/building-authority.html).

## 6. Close gaps with real pieces

Use [Content Opportunities](../insights/content-opportunities.html), and write
one substantial piece per cluster rather than a page per question.

---

## A reasonable first quarter

Weeks 1–2: set up, run, read.
Weeks 3–4: make the top ten existing pages quotable.
Weeks 5–8: fix how you are described where it is wrong.
Weeks 9–12: one or two substantial pieces for the biggest gaps, then measure.
