# Improve AI Visibility for an Ecommerce Brand

A worked sequence for a retailer who wants to be recommended when somebody asks
an assistant what to buy.

---

## 1. Set the brand up as buyers think of it

One brand per market. A shop selling in two countries has two sets of
questions, competitors and sources, and merging them produces one blurred
average.

See [Create Your First Brand](../getting-started/create-your-first-brand.html).

## 2. Build topics from purchase questions

Not product categories — the questions people ask before buying. "Best running
shoes for flat feet" is a topic. "Footwear" is not.

Pull from what customers ask support, and from Search Console if connected.

## 3. Run, then read the answers

Read before you look at scores. For retail you are checking three things:

- Are you named at all?
- Are specific **products** named, or just the shop?
- What is cited — review sites, YouTube, Reddit, retailer guides?

## 4. Work the product layer

Retail has a step software does not: individual products appear in answers.
[Product Visibility](../shopping/product-visibility.html) shows which of yours
do, and the [agentic commerce](../agentic-commerce/agentic-commerce-readiness.html)
readiness work matters here — structured, complete product data is what makes a
product quotable.

## 5. Fix the sources, not just the site

Ecommerce answers lean heavily on third parties: reviews, comparison guides,
creator videos. [Channels and Authors](../insights/channels-and-authors.html)
names the specific YouTube channels and subreddits shaping your category. That
is an outreach list.

## 6. Measure the thing you care about

"Be recommended for *[your category]* on ChatGPT in the UK" is a
[Business Scorecard](../scorecard/index.html) goal. Visibility across all
topics is not.

---

## What usually moves the needle

In order: accurate and complete product data; presence on the review sites
already cited; one genuinely useful buying guide that answers a cluster of
questions; and correcting third-party pages that describe you wrongly.
