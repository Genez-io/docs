# Introduction

People increasingly ask an assistant instead of searching. If your brand is not
in the answer, you lost the moment — even if you rank first on Google for the
same question.

This section explains what that changes and how Genezio measures it.

**Start here** if you are new: [What is Genezio?](what-is-genezio.html), then
[Genezio for Marketers](genezio-for-marketers.html) for the business case, or
[How LLM Search Works](how-llm-search-works.html) for the mechanics.

The two numbers everything else rests on are **AI Visibility** (do you appear)
and **AI Recommendation** (are you put forward). They move independently, and
the gap between them is usually the most useful thing on your dashboard.
