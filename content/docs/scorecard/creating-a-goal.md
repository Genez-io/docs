# Creating a Goal

You create a goal by describing it, in a conversation. You do not configure
metrics, pick dimensions or write a formula.

---

## Start with a sentence

Open the Business Scorecard for your brand and start a new goal. Say what you
want in plain language:

> I want to be the brand ChatGPT recommends when someone asks for a project
> management tool for agencies.

The agent reads your brand — its topics, competitors, personas and the answer
engines you track — and works out what to measure. Where something is genuinely
ambiguous it asks, and it asks about the business, not about settings.

---

## Narrow it as you go

The conversation continues until the goal is right. You can add constraints at
any point:

> Only for the UK market.

> Compare us against Asana and Monday, not the whole market.

> Just the topics where someone is close to buying.

Each time, the agent rebuilds the goal and shows you what it would track. When
the card matches what you meant, accept it.

---

## You never read code

A goal is measured by a small program the agent writes. You never see it, and
you never have to. You are shown what the goal measures in words, and you
accept or keep refining.

If a goal ever looks wrong, you say so in the same conversation and the agent
changes it.

---

## Your draft is kept

If you close the tab halfway through, your draft is still there when you come
back. A goal that took a long conversation is not lost to a stray reload.

---

## Changing a goal later

The conversation does not end when the goal is created. Open the goal and keep
talking to it: widen the market, swap a competitor, change the engines. The
goal carries on from the history it already has.
