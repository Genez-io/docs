# Business Scorecard

Visibility numbers tell you what is happening. The Business Scorecard tells you
whether it is what **you** wanted.

A scorecard holds your goals — the handful of things you actually care about
this quarter — and tracks each one every day. Instead of reading six dashboards
and deciding for yourself whether the quarter is going well, you open one board
and see each goal where it stands.

---

## Why it exists

Two brands can have the same visibility score and be in completely different
positions. One is winning the topics that lead to a sale; the other is winning
topics nobody buys from. A general metric cannot tell them apart. A goal can,
because you said what mattered.

A goal is written in your own words, such as:

- *Be recommended for "best CRM for small teams" on ChatGPT and Perplexity.*
- *Appear in more answers than our two closest competitors on pricing topics.*
- *Be cited by the review sites our buyers read.*

The scorecard then measures exactly that, every day, and shows you the trend.

---

## What a goal gives you

**A number you chose.** Not a platform average — the thing you said you wanted.

**A history.** Each goal records where it stood yesterday, so you can see
movement rather than a single reading.

**Its working.** Open a card and you can see where the number came from, down
to the conversations behind it. Nothing is a black box you have to trust.

**A category and the data it reads.** Each goal says what kind of goal it is
and which parts of your brand data it draws on, so anybody opening the board
understands it without asking you.
