# Managing Your Board

A board is read far more often than it is edited, so it is worth keeping it to
the goals that matter now.

---

## Order

Drag the cards into the order you want to read them. The order is yours and it
is kept, so the goal you check first every morning stays at the top.

---

## Tags

Tag a goal to group it — by market, by campaign, by the team that owns it, or
by the quarter it belongs to. Tags can be changed or removed at any time, and
they are the quickest way to keep a board readable once it holds more than a
handful of goals.

---

## Renaming

The title of a goal can be changed from the goal's own page. Rename freely:
the measurement does not depend on the title.

---

## Archiving

A goal you no longer track can be taken off the board. It goes to the archive
rather than being deleted, with its history intact, and can be restored to the
board later.

That matters for seasonal work. A goal about a campaign can be archived when
the campaign ends and brought back next year with last year's trend still
attached.

---

## Who changed what

Every action on a scorecard is recorded in the audit log — goals created,
changed, archived and restored. If a goal moves and nobody remembers touching
it, the log answers the question.
