# Analyzing Many URLs at Once

You rarely want to analyze one page. You want to know which of your forty
product pages are weak, or how a competitor's whole blog compares with yours.

Paste a list of URLs and each one becomes its own analysis.

---

## How it works

1. Open the Content Analyzer.
2. Paste the addresses, one per line, as full URLs
   (`https://example.com/your-article`).
3. Start the run.

Each page gets **its own analysis and its own report**, so you can open any
single result in full rather than reading an average. A run takes up to a fixed
number of URLs; the analyzer tells you if your list is over it.

---

## What it costs

Each page counts separately against your daily analysis limit — ten URLs is ten
analyses, not one. The analyzer shows you the count before you start, so you
can decide whether to run the whole list today or split it.

If some addresses are malformed, you are told which, and the valid ones still
run.

---

## What it is good for

**Auditing a section of your site.** Analyze every page in one category and
sort by the weakest. That turns "our content needs work" into a specific list
of pages and a specific reason for each.

**Comparing against a competitor.** Run their pages beside yours on the same
topics and see where they are more quotable.

**Checking a migration.** After a redesign, re-run the same list and compare.
A redesign that increased page furniture at the expense of prose shows up
immediately.
