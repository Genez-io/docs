# Chatting with Your Article

Every article in Content Hub has a conversation attached. You can ask for
changes in words instead of editing by hand.

---

## What it is good for

**Changes that cut across the whole piece.** "Make this for practitioners, not
executives" touches every paragraph. Describing it once is faster than
rewriting each.

**Filling a gap you noticed.** "Add a section on what this costs for a team of
five" — the writer has the brief, the sources and the fanouts already.

**Checking the piece against its purpose.** "Which of our target questions does
this not answer yet?" is a useful thing to ask before publishing.

---

## What it is not good for

Small, exact edits. Fixing a word, a number or a link is faster by hand. See
[Editing Articles](editing-articles.html).

---

## Attachments

You can attach files to the conversation, so a style guide, a product sheet or
a research document becomes material the writer works from. Attached files are
kept with the piece, so it stays clear later what the article was built on.

---

## Your language

The conversation is in your brand's language. You brief and read in the same
language the piece is written in. See
[Working in Your Brand Language](working-in-your-brand-language.html).

---

## A good habit

After any substantial change, re-read one passage and apply the citation test:
does it still answer a question on its own? Broad instructions sometimes smooth
a piece into something that reads well and answers less.
