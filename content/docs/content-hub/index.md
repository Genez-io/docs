# Content Hub

Creating content that answer engines will actually cite — and analysing what
you already have.

**Writing:** start [from the data](from-data-to-content-strategy.html), build a
[brief](briefs.html) from [query fanouts](using-query-fanouts-for-content.html),
then [generate](generating-articles.html), [edit](editing-articles.html) and
[publish](publishing-content.html).

**Analysing:** the [Content Analyzer](content-analyzer.html) judges a page
against the questions your buyers ask — including
[landing pages](analyzing-landing-pages.html), which are read differently, and
[whole lists of URLs](analyzing-many-urls-at-once.html).

One test runs through this whole section: can a paragraph of the page answer
the question on its own? Engines lift passages, and a page with nothing
liftable is not cited however well it reads.
