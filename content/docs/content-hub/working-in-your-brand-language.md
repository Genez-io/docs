# Working in Your Brand Language

Content Hub works in your brand's language, not in English with a translation
step at the end.

---

## What this means in practice

**The conversation.** When you chat with Content Hub about a brief or an
article, it answers in your brand's language. You are not switching languages
to brief a writer and back again to read the result.

**The content analysis report.** The analysis of a page is reported in your
brand's language, so a report can go to a local team without being rewritten.

---

## Why it matters beyond convenience

Content written in English and then translated loses the thing that earns
citations: the vocabulary people actually use when they ask. "Best CRM for
small teams" and its equivalent in Romanian are not the same phrase translated
— they carry different words, and answer engines match on those words.

Working in the brand's language from the start keeps the piece aligned with the
demand Genezio measured, rather than aligned with an English version of it.

---

## Setting it

The language comes from the brand. Set it on the brand and everything
downstream follows — the conversation, the writing and the analysis.

A brand that works across several markets is usually better as several brands,
one per market, so each has its own language, topics and competitors.
