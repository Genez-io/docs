# Content Opportunities

A content opportunity is a question your buyers ask answer engines where you
have no presence and somebody else does.

---

## How they are found

Genezio already knows three things: the questions being asked in your category,
which of them you appear in, and what the engines cite when they answer. An
opportunity is where those line up badly — real demand, a competitor present,
you absent.

That is a sharper starting point than a keyword list, because it begins from
answers that are already being given rather than from search volume.

---

## What makes one worth taking

**The question is genuinely being asked.** It came from real conversations, not
from a guess about what people might search.

**A competitor is winning it.** Somebody being recommended proves an answer
engine is willing to recommend *someone* here.

**You can credibly be the answer.** The honest filter. If a question is about a
capability you do not have, visibility will not help and may harm you.

---

## Turning one into work

Open the conversations behind the opportunity and read what the engine said.
Look at what it cited — that is the material currently doing the job, and it
tells you the shape of what would beat it.

From there, Content Hub can take a topic straight into a brief, with the
fanouts and the sources attached. See
[From Data to Content Strategy](../content-hub/from-data-to-content-strategy.html).

---

## What not to do

Do not write a page for every opportunity. Answer engines lift passages, and a
thin page written to cover a gap gives them nothing to lift. One substantial
piece that genuinely answers a cluster of related questions beats five pages
that each half-answer one.
