# Competitor Insights

Your own numbers tell you how you are doing. Competitor insights tell you
whether that is good.

Genezio runs the same conversations for your competitors as for you, so every
metric you have for your brand you also have for theirs, measured the same way
on the same questions.

---

## What you can see

**Who appears where you do not.** The most useful list in the product: topics
where competitors are visible and you are not. Each one is a question your
buyers are asking that you have no presence in.

**Who is recommended when you are only mentioned.** A competitor with lower
visibility but higher recommendation is beating you where it counts. They
appear less often and convert the appearance better.

**How the field moves.** Competitor scores over time show whether a change in
your position is you improving or the category shifting around you.

---

## Where competitors come from

Genezio extracts competitors from the answers themselves — the brands engines
actually name beside you — rather than from a list you supply. See
[Extracting Competitors](../analysis/extracting-competitors.html).

That matters, because the competitors an answer engine puts beside you are not
always the ones your sales team names. Discovering a brand you had not
considered is a common and useful outcome.

You can also add competitors you want tracked regardless.

---

## Using it

The practical loop is narrow:

1. Find a topic where a competitor is visible and you are not.
2. Open the conversations and read what the engine said about them.
3. Look at what it cited. That is the material doing the work.
4. Decide whether you can be a better source for that question.

That is a far more specific brief than "improve our content".
