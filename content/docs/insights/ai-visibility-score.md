# AI Visibility Score

**AI Visibility** answers one question: when someone asks an answer engine about
your category, how often does your brand come up at all?

> **Formula:** conversations where your brand appears ÷ total eligible
> conversations × 100

A brand at 80% appears in four of every five relevant answers. A brand at 15%
is invisible for most of the category.

---

## What it does not tell you

Appearing is not the same as being recommended. You can be mentioned in an
answer that goes on to recommend somebody else — named as the expensive
option, or as the one the buyer has outgrown.

That is why this number is always read beside
[AI Recommendation](../core-concepts/brand-recommendation.html). High visibility
with low recommendation is a positioning problem, not an awareness problem, and
the fix is different.

---

## What moves it

Visibility rises when an engine has more reason to bring you into an answer:

- **More sources that mention you.** Engines draw on what they can find. A
  brand discussed in reviews, comparisons and community threads has more routes
  into an answer than one discussed only on its own website.
- **Coverage of the questions people actually ask.** Appearing for "best CRM"
  and nowhere else leaves most of the category uncovered.
- **Being unambiguous.** A brand whose name collides with a common word or
  another company loses mentions to the collision.

---

## Reading it honestly

**Compare against your competitors, not an industry number.** 40% in a crowded
category can be strong; 40% in a niche with three players is weak. The score
only means something next to the brands you actually compete with.

**Look at it per topic.** One number for a brand hides the shape. You are
usually strong on some topics and absent on others, and the absent ones are the
work.

**Expect movement.** Answer engines change their answers. A few points either
way between runs is normal; a sustained slide is not.
