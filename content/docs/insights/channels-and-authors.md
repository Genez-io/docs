# Channels and Authors

A citation tells you which site an answer engine leaned on. That is often not
specific enough to act on.

"YouTube was cited" is not useful. "*This* channel was cited, eleven times, in
answers about your category" is — because a channel has an owner you can
approach. The same is true of Reddit: the site tells you nothing, the
**subreddit** tells you where the conversation about you is happening.

Genezio resolves citations down to the channel and the author behind them.

---

## Where to find it

Open any citation and look at the domain. Where Genezio can identify who
published the cited material, the drawer shows **Channels & authors** with how
often each one appeared.

You get:

- **Channels** — the YouTube channel or the subreddit, not just the domain.
- **Authors** — the person credited with the piece, where the source names one.

Each is counted, so you can tell a single mention from a pattern.

---

## What to do with it

**Find the creators who shape your category.** If three answers about you cite
the same YouTube channel, that channel is part of how AI describes your market.
It is a far more precise target than "do some YouTube marketing".

**Find the communities that matter.** A subreddit that keeps appearing is where
your buyers are already discussing the problem you solve. Knowing which one is
the difference between a vague social plan and showing up where it counts.

**Spot a single voice carrying a lot of weight.** If one author is behind
several citations, your reputation in AI answers is partly in their hands —
worth knowing, whether what they write is flattering or not.

**Prioritise outreach by evidence.** A channel cited eleven times is a better
use of a week than one cited once.

---

## A note on coverage

Not every citation can be attributed. A source has to identify its channel or
author for Genezio to resolve one, so the panel appears where that information
exists and stays out of the way where it does not. Think of it as a sharper
view of part of your citations rather than a replacement for the citation
reporting as a whole.
