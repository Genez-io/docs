# Insights

Turning the measurements into decisions.

[Your KPIs Explained](your-kpis-explained.html) defines the three numbers and
how they relate. [AI Visibility Score](ai-visibility-score.html) and
[Share of Voice](share-of-voice.html) cover two of them in depth.

For deciding what to do next:
[Most Cited Sources](most-cited-sources.html) is the most actionable report in
the product, [Content Opportunities](content-opportunities.html) turns gaps
into briefs, and [Competitor Insights](competitor-insights.html) tells you
whether your numbers are good.

For reputation: [sentiment](sentiment-analysis.html) gives the temperature,
[perceptions](ai-perception-summary.html) give the claim, and
[Monitor Perceptions](monitor-perceptions.html) tracks whether it changes.
