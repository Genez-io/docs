# Google Analytics Integration

Connect Google Analytics 4 and Genezio can set what answer engines say about
you beside what happens when people arrive.

---

## What it adds

Analytics describes behaviour on your site: which pages people reach, what
they do next. Genezio describes what happens *before* that — whether you were
in the answer at all.

Together they close a gap neither closes alone. A page cited often in AI
answers that converts nobody is a different problem from a page nobody is
cited for.

---

## Connecting it

1. Open the brand's **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Connect the Google account with access to the property.
3. Choose the property for this brand.

---

## What it is not

Analytics cannot tell you why somebody arrived. Most answer engines send
little or no referrer information, so traffic that began as an AI
recommendation often looks direct.

This is worth being clear about internally: **you cannot attribute AI
visibility through Analytics**, and a team expecting to will conclude the work
is not paying off when the data simply is not there.

Treat the two as complementary measures, not as one funnel.

---

## The more useful connection

For most teams [Google Search Console](google-search-console.html) is the
stronger integration, because its queries describe *demand* — what people
actually ask — which can be turned into topics. Analytics describes behaviour
after the fact.
