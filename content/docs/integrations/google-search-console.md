# Google Search Console

Connect Search Console and Genezio can use what people actually searched for
on your site, alongside what they ask answer engines.

---

## What you get

**Topics from real demand.** Genezio can read your Search Console queries and
generate topics from them. Instead of guessing what to track, you start from
the phrases that already bring people to you.

That matters because the gap between the two is the interesting part: a query
that brings you traffic from search but never appears in AI answers is exactly
where you are losing ground.

**Your search data beside your AI data.** The queries sit next to the questions
answer engines are asked, so you can see where the two agree and where they
diverge.

---

## Connecting it

1. Open the brand's **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Connect the Google account that has access to the property.
3. Choose the property for this brand.

A **domain property** reads the whole site. A URL-prefix property reads only
the addresses under that prefix, so pick the one that matches the brand.

---

## Generating topics from queries

Once connected, you can generate topics from the queries Search Console
reports. The queries are filtered by default to the ones worth acting on —
you are not handed every long-tail string your site has ever matched.

Review what it proposes before accepting. Search queries and AI questions are
not the same shape: "crm pricing" is a search, "what does a CRM cost for a team
of five" is what somebody asks an assistant. The generated topics bridge that,
and they are better for a quick read-through.

---

## Google Analytics 4

Analytics 4 can be connected from the same place. It describes how a website
behaves rather than what people ask, so it supports the picture rather than
driving topics.
