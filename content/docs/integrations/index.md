# Integrations

Connecting Genezio with the rest of your stack.

[Google Search Console](google-search-console.html) is the most useful
connection for most teams: its queries describe real demand, and topics can be
generated from them.
[Google Analytics](google-analytics-integration.html) adds behaviour after the
click — with one important limit, which that page is explicit about.

[CDN Log Integration](cdn-log-integration.html) shows which answer-engine
crawlers reach your site.

To get data out: [Data Exports](data-exports.html) for one-off PDF and CSV, and
[External Reporting Tools](external-reporting-tools.html) for anything you will
repeat, which should go through the [API](../api/index.html).
