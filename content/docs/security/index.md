# Security & Data

How Genezio handles access, identity and the data in your account.

[Enterprise SSO and SCIM](enterprise-sso-and-scim.html) covers signing in
through your own identity provider and provisioning members automatically.

Access inside an account is controlled two ways: a member can be limited to
specific brands rather than the whole account, and an
[API key](../api/authentication.html) can be scoped the same way, so an
integration only reaches the brands it needs.

For the policy pages in this section — data collection, retention, privacy,
certifications and compliance — contact your account manager or
`security@genezio.com`. Those are formal commitments and are answered
directly rather than summarised here.
